Quasaria Beacons

Lock QFX into one of six tiers and share real protocol revenue, paid in XLM every 4 hours. Every QFX earns the same share; tiers unlock perks. Play with the numbers below. Rates are variable and not guaranteed.

Open the simulator →

Tiers, priced in QFX

QFX is pegged 1:1 to XLM and fully backed. Tier weight is flat (1.00) in every tier, so the same QFX with the same lock earns the same share. Tiers unlock perks only. Optional lock boosts are open to everyone.

Lock boost (any tier): none 1.00× · 30 days 1.10× · 90 days 1.25×. Perks also include Constellation XP and tier visuals. No per-wallet reward cap at launch.

Blend pool choice

40% of staked value (range 30–50%) is supplied to a Blend lending pool; 60% stays as a cash buffer for exits.

Live-rate caveat: Blend's XLM supply rate is variable. It was about 0.02% on a 30-day average and under 0.01% spot (DefiLlama, Oct 11, 2026), versus ~9% including emissions earlier in 2026. The simulator defaults to 0.5%, an illustrative base case. At today's rates almost all Beacons yield (~96%) comes from trading fees.

Reward schedule

Revenue flows into a reserve. Each 4-hour cycle pays out a slice of it, so payouts are smooth and never jump.

1/42 every 4 hours

Each cycle pays out 1/42 of the reserve (≈ 7 days of smoothing), shared by weight. Claim anytime or let the keeper auto-claim daily.

25% APR cap

A cycle never pays more than 25% a year on staked value (25% × staked ÷ 2,190). Anything above the cap stays in the reserve.

30-day overflow rule

Reserve above 30 days of payouts flows to insurance (until it holds 5% of the Blend deployment), then to buyback/treasury.

Peg monitor

If QFX drifts more than 1% from XLM, new stakes and tier upgrades pause automatically. Claims and exits keep working.

Unbonding rules

  • 7-day unbonding (42 cycles). No rewards while unbonding. The keeper pulls XLM back from Blend during this window.
  • Exit cap: at most 2% of staked value is released per 4-hour cycle (12% a day).
  • FIFO queue: first in, first out, with your position and ETA shown. Exits are never paid from other stakers' rewards.
  • One-epoch warm-up: new stake starts earning after one full 4-hour cycle.
  • After an uncovered Blend loss: a pro-rata haircut applies to every exit, so leaving first gives no advantage.
  • If QFX minting is paused: exits are paid in XLM until it resumes (redeeming can never be paused).

How staked QFX reaches Blend (Option B)

The vault redeems your QFX for XLM 1:1 (burning it), lends 40% on Blend's XLM pool, keeps 60% as a buffer, and mints fresh QFX 1:1 for you on exit. The QFX peg reserve stays fully backed for all circulating QFX.

Why not Option A (lending the peg reserve's own XLM)? It would need a risky QFX contract upgrade, break the built-in backing check, and put Blend risk on every QFX holder instead of only stakers who opted in.
